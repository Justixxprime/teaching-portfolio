How she works

A process built around the individual child.

Observation

Time spent understanding the child before any plan is written, including how they communicate, what motivates them, and where they find things difficult.

Personalised planning

Goals set with the family, not imposed on them, based on what actually matters for that child's day to day life.

Consistent one to one work

Regular sessions that build a relationship of trust, adapting as the child's needs and confidence change.

Reflection with the family

Ongoing conversations with parents about what is working, what needs adjusting, and what progress looks like next.

A closer look

What one session actually looks like.

01
The environment

A space prepared in advance, calm and free of distraction, set up around what today's session needs.

02
The connection

The first few minutes are rarely about the task itself. They're about the child feeling safe enough to begin.

03
The activity

Chosen for this child specifically: what motivates them, what stretches them, and what they're ready for today.

04
The process

Progress rarely looks dramatic in the moment. It looks like sustained attention, a second attempt, a small adjustment.

05
The progress

What gets recorded afterward: what worked, what to try next, and what to tell the family.
