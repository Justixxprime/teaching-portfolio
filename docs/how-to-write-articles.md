# How to Write and Publish an Article

## To write a new article
1. Go to **yoursite.com/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub** (use the account your developer set up for you)
3. Click **New Insight Article**
4. Fill in:
   - **Title** — the article's headline
   - **Category** — pick the closest match from the dropdown
   - **Publish Date** — today's date, or whenever you want it dated
   - **Short Description** — one or two sentences that summarize the article. This is what people see before clicking into it.
   - **Article Body** — the article itself. Use the toolbar above the text box for bold, headings, bullet points, and links, just like a normal document editor.
5. Click **Publish** in the top right.

That's it. Your article goes live on the Insights page within a minute or two, no need to tell your developer or wait for anything else.

## To edit an existing article
1. Log in the same way.
2. Click the article in the list on the left.
3. Make your changes.
4. Click **Publish** again to update it live.

## To delete an article
1. Open the article.
2. Look for **Delete entry** (usually near the Publish button).
3. Confirm.

## A few tips
- Write like you're talking to a parent, not writing a textbook. That's the tone the rest of the site uses.
- Keep the Short Description honest and specific, it's what convinces someone to click.
- If you're not sure whether something reads well, save it and come back to it later with fresh eyes before publishing.

## If something goes wrong
If the page shows an error when you try to log in or publish, that usually means something on the technical setup needs a look, not something you did wrong. Reach out to your developer.
