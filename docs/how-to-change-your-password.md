# How to Change Your Website's Private Area Password

You can do this yourself, anytime, in under a minute. You do not need to contact your developer.

## To change the password

1. Go to **supabase.com** and log in with the account your developer set up for you.
2. Click **Table Editor** on the left side of the screen.
3. Click on **site_access** (it's the only table you'll see).
4. You'll see one row with the current password in it. Click directly on that text.
5. Type the new password you want to use.
6. Press **Enter** on your keyboard.

That's it. The new password works on the website immediately — no waiting, no need to tell anyone, nothing else to click.

## To give someone access

Just share the password with them directly (by text, email, or however you'd normally share it) along with the website address for the private area. Anyone with the current password can get in.

## If you want to remove everyone's access at once

Simply change the password (steps above). Everyone who had the old password will need the new one to get back in — this is the easiest way to "lock out" people who shouldn't have access anymore, since there's one shared password for everyone rather than individual logins.

## Questions

If anything above doesn't work as described, or the page shows an error, reach out to your developer, that likely means the technical setup needs a look, not something on your end.
