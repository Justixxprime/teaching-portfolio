Schools vary widely in how much experience they have supporting children with additional needs, and that can be frustrating for parents navigating it for the first time. A good starting point is treating the relationship as a partnership rather than a negotiation.

Bringing specific, observable information helps: what works at home, what triggers difficulty, what small accommodations have made a difference. Vague requests are harder for teachers to act on than concrete ones.
