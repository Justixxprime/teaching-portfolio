Case study

Finding a Rhythm: Building Communication Through Play

Context, goals, approach and outcome for one child, shared with permission.

The story

Context

A 6 year old referred after struggling to communicate needs in a mainstream classroom setting.

Goals

Consistent use of a few key phrases to express needs, and fewer frustrated outbursts during transitions.

Approach

Weekly sessions built around the child's specific interests, gradually introducing structured communication tools within play.

Outcome

Within a few months, the family reported fewer meltdowns at home and a child who initiated communication far more often.

Reflection

A reminder that communication support works best when it's woven into what a child already loves doing.
