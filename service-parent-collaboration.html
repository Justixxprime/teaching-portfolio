Service

Parent Collaboration

Parents are part of the process, not observers of it. This service formalises that: regular structured conversations, not just updates in passing.

Who this is for

Every family she works with. This isn't an optional add-on, it's built into how sessions are planned from the start.

What it involves

Regular check-ins about what's working at home, what's difficult, and how goals should shift.

How feedback flows both ways

Parents share what they're noticing day to day; she shares what she's noticing in sessions. Plans adjust based on both.
