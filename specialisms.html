Specialisms

Four areas, each approached on its own terms.

01 · Autism

Meeting each child's communication style directly, rather than expecting them to adapt to a one size fits all approach.

What it means: communication style, routine, and sensory needs vary widely between children, so support starts with observation, not assumption.

Her experience and approach here: Sessions typically focus on communication tools, routine building, and gradually expanding comfort with new environments..

02 · Down syndrome

Supporting each child's development at their own pace, with a strong focus on communication and everyday independence.

Developmental and academic support suited to each child's own pace and communication style.

Her experience and approach here: Work often centers on speech and language goals, fine motor activities, and building confidence in social settings..

03 · Developmental delays

Starting from exactly where a child is right now, and building forward without comparing them to a fixed timeline.

Observation led support that builds gradually from exactly where the child is now, not from a fixed benchmark.

Her experience and approach here: Sessions are built around close observation first, with goals adjusted regularly as the child's needs become clearer..

04 · Learning difficulties

Academic support shaped around how a specific child actually learns, not a generic curriculum applied uniformly.

Individual academic support suited to how a specific child learns best, not a generic curriculum applied uniformly.

Her experience and approach here: Focus areas often include reading confidence, structured routines for homework, and rebuilding a child's relationship with learning..
